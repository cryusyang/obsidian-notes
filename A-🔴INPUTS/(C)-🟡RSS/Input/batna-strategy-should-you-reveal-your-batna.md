![BATNA](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/wp-content/uploads/images/posts/batna.jpg)

In their best-selling book [*Getting to Yes: Negotiating Agreement Without Giving In*](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/shop/getting-to-yes-negotiating-agreement-without-giving-in/#:~:text=Getting%20to%20YES%20offers%20a,or%20corporations%2C%20tenants%20or%20diplomats.), Roger Fisher, William Ury, and Bruce Patton introduced the idea of a BATNA (best alternative to a negotiated agreement), describing it as “the standard against which any proposed agreement should be measured.” When you’re clear on what you’ll do if a deal isn’t reached, you can use that baseline to evaluate the proposals on the table and make more confident, informed decisions.

在他们畅销的著作《谈判力：不屈服的达成协议之道》（*Getting to Yes: Negotiating Agreement Without Giving In*）中，罗杰·费希尔（Roger Fisher）、威廉·尤里（William Ury）和布鲁斯·巴顿（Bruce Patton）提出了“BATNA”（达成协议的最佳替代方案）这一概念，并将其定义为“衡量任何拟议协议的标准”。当你清楚若协议无法达成时自己将采取何种行动，你便能以此为基准评估摆在桌面上的各项提议，从而做出更自信、更明智的决策。

Generally speaking, the better your [BATNA](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/batna/translate-your-batna-to-the-current-deal/) strategy, the greater your bargaining power. Suppose you have received an appealing job offer from another company. When [negotiating with your boss](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/negotiating-with-your-boss/) for a promotion, you can ask assertively for what you would like without worrying about what will happen if your boss can’t meet your needs. Awareness of your BATNA will keep you from accepting a worse outcome than you could get elsewhere—and from rejecting an agreement that’s better than your BATNA.

一般来说，你的BATNA策略越强，你的议价能力就越强。假设你已收到另一家公司的诱人工作邀约；那么，在与上司就晋升事宜进行谈判时，你便可坚定地提出自己的诉求，而无需担忧上司若无法满足你的要求时会如何——因为你心里有底。对自身BATNA的清醒认知，既可防止你接受一个比其他选择更差的结果，也可避免你拒绝一项优于BATNA的协议。

Negotiators often wonder whether they should reveal their BATNA to their counterpart and, if they do, at what point in the negotiation process should they do so. The following six do’s and don’ts will help you manage your BATNA strategy with confidence.

谈判者常常困惑：是否应向对方透露自己的BATNA？倘若决定透露，又该在谈判进程的哪个阶段为之？以下六条“宜做”与“忌做”建议，将助你自信地运筹BATNA策略。

### Six BATNA Strategy Guidelines  
### 六条BATNA策略指南

1. **Don’t reveal a weak BATNA.**  
1. **切勿透露薄弱的BATNA。**

It’s Negotiation 101: Never share your BATNA with the other party if it is hopelessly weak. A bad BATNA is also known as a [WATNA](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/watna/), or *worst alternative to a negotiated agreement*. Telling a supplier, for example, that you lost your last partner and are desperate to do a new deal is a surefire way to ensure the supplier will highball you on price. Avoid appearing to be in a hurry, seeming stressed, or revealing that you have a wide-open schedule—all potential “tells” that you are eager to close a deal.

这是谈判入门常识：若你的BATNA明显孱弱不堪，切勿向对方透露。糟糕的BATNA亦称作WATNA（达成协议的最差替代方案）。例如，若你告诉供应商“上一家合作伙伴已终止合作，我急迫需要新协议”，无异于明示对方可大幅抬高报价。因此，请避免表现得匆忙、焦虑，或透露自己日程空闲——这些都可能成为暴露你急于成交的“破绽”。

2. **Don’t bluff about your BATNA.**  
2. **切勿就BATNA虚张声势。**

What if the other side asks you about your BATNA directly? Explain (truthfully) that you are working on various possibilities but want to concentrate on the deal on the table for the time being. Resist the urge to embellish or fabricate a BATNA to try to boost your bargaining power. You’ll compromise your ethics and reputation if you’re caught in an exaggeration or a misrepresentation of facts, or if you lie.

倘若对方直接询问你的BATNA，该如何应对？请如实说明：你正在探索多种可能性，但现阶段希望聚焦于当前谈判议题。切勿为提升议价能力而粉饰甚至捏造BATNA。一旦被发现夸大其词、歪曲事实或撒谎，你的职业操守与信誉将严重受损。

3. **Don’t reveal your BATNA too early.**  
3. **切勿过早透露BATNA。**

Even if you’re certain your BATNA strategy is rock solid, hold off on revealing it. That’s because the information could come across as a threat: “If you can’t give me an even better deal than the one I just outlined, I’m out of here.” [Threats in negotiation](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/how-to-make-wise-threats/) foster a competitive atmosphere and hinder your ability to explore value-creating trade-offs.

即便你确信自己的BATNA坚不可摧，也请暂缓透露。因为此举易被解读为威胁：“若你们无法提供比我刚提出的条件更优的方案，我就退出谈判。” 谈判中的威胁会激化对抗氛围，阻碍双方发掘创造价值的互惠交换机会。

Moreover, if the other party doesn’t think your BATNA is as strong as you do, they might drive a harder bargain than they would if they didn’t know about your best alternative. The other party might even try to worsen your BATNA. For example, if you tell a potential employer about a great offer you have that’s due to expire in 24 hours, they might try to drag out talks to allow the deadline to pass.

此外，倘若对方并不认为你的BATNA如你所想般强劲，他们反而可能比不知情时更加强硬地压价。对方甚至可能试图削弱你的BATNA。例如，若你向潜在雇主透露自己手握一份24小时内即将失效的优质邀约，对方可能故意拖延谈判，坐等截止期限到来。

Finally, keep in mind that a strong BATNA could be a useful bargaining chip near the end of a negotiation after you’ve exhausted other strategies.

最后，请谨记：一个强有力的BATNA，往往在谈判尾声、其他策略均已用尽之时，才真正成为一枚关键的议价筹码。

4. **Do work to actively improve your BATNA.**  
4. **务必积极提升你的BATNA。**

It’s not enough to simply talk about your BATNA. Rather, you need to do everything you can to try to improve it. For a job seeker, that might mean continuing to seek leads in your network or thinking about other paths, such as going back to school.

仅谈论BATNA远远不够；你必须竭尽所能去切实提升它。对求职者而言，这或许意味着持续在人际网络中拓展机会，或思考其他路径，例如重返校园深造。

5. **Don’t let them talk you out of your BATNA.**  
5. **切勿让对方动摇你对BATNA的信心。**

Suppose you’re thrilled with your BATNA strategy and are eager to play it up: “I’ve got a great deal waiting for me at the Subaru dealership down the street, but I wanted to see what Honda had to offer first.” Don’t be surprised if your counterpart starts telling you about the unreliable Subaru they had 10 years ago and how they’d never buy one again. Of course, it’s smart to investigate any potentially legitimate claims the person makes about your BATNA, but recognize that they have very real incentives to try to convince you that your outside options are not as good as you’d like to believe.

假设你对自己的BATNA策略信心十足，甚至主动提及：“我家附近斯巴鲁（Subaru）经销商已为我备好一份极佳的购车方案，但我还是想先看看本田（Honda）能提供什么。” 若此时对方开始向你讲述十年前那辆不可靠的斯巴鲁，并断言自己绝不再购斯巴鲁——请勿惊讶。当然，对于对方关于你BATNA提出的任何看似合理之说，审慎查证是明智之举；但请清醒认识到：对方有非常现实的动机，试图说服你相信：你的外部选择远不如你自以为的那般理想。

6. **Do encourage them to share their BATNA.**  
6. **不妨鼓励对方透露其BATNA。**

Negotiators are sometimes reluctant to ask counterparts about their BATNA for fear of discovering that it’s impossible to compete with. In fact, this would be knowledge to seek out rather than to shy away from. If there’s no way you can give them what they can get from someone else, revealing this fact and ending the negotiation would save everyone time. On the flip side, your queries about your counterpart’s BATNA could reveal that they have a terrible one—valuable information that will put you in a stronger [bargaining position](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/freemium/batna-basics-boost-your-power-at-the-bargaining-table/).

谈判者有时不愿向对方探询其BATNA，唯恐得知对方拥有自己根本无力匹敌的替代方案。事实上，这类信息恰恰值得主动探寻，而非避之不及。倘若你确实无法提供对方从别处可轻易获得的条件，坦诚相告并及时终止谈判，反而是对各方时间的尊重与节约。反之，你对对方BATNA的提问，也可能意外揭示其BATNA极其糟糕——这一宝贵信息将显著增强你的[议价地位](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/freemium/batna-basics-boost-your-power-at-the-bargaining-table/)。
