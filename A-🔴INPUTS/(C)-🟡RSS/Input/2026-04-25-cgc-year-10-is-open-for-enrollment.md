<p><a href="https://stevepavlina.com/conscious-growth-club/"><strong>Conscious Growth Club Year 10</strong></a> is now open for enrollment.</p>



<p>CGC is a live, year-long growth space for people who want deeper connection, real support, clearer direction, and a life that feels better to actually live.</p>



<p>This year’s CGC structure is designed to be simpler, more participatory, and more closely attuned to what members are actually moving through. You&#8217;ll get several dozen live Zoom calls throughout the year, access to the private CGC forum, and access to the full CGC content library.</p>



<p>CGC works best for people who are willing to show up live, be on camera, participate honestly, and help create a warm, high-trust room together.</p>



<p>This is not mainly a content library to consume privately. It’s a living space with real people, real conversations, and real support over time.</p>



<p>CGC Year 10 officially runs from May 1, 2026 through April 30, 2027.</p>



<p>Enrollment closes Thursday, May 7 at 11:59 PM Pacific.</p>
