Galinsky and New York University professor Joe C. Magee.</p>
<h3>Negotiation Power &#8211; Three Main Sources</h3>
<p style="padding-left: 30px;"><strong>1. A strong <a href="https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/batna/">BATNA</a>.</strong> Your <em>best alternative to a negotiated agreement</em>, or BATNA, is often your best source of bargaining power. By cultivating a strong outside alternative, you gain the power you need to walk away from an unappealing deal. For example, a home buyer could improve her power in a negotiation with a seller by finding another house she likes just as much.</p>
<p style="padding-left: 30px;"><strong>2. Role power.</strong> Power can come from a strong role, title, or position, such as a high rank in an organization. When negotiating with your boss, for instance, you sometimes may need to cede to his preferences because of his high status.</p>
<p style="padding-left: 30px;"><strong>3. Psychological power.</strong> Negotiators can bring a sense of psychological power to the table—the feeling that they’re powerful, whether or not that’s objectively the case. Simply thinking about a time in your life when you had power can bolster your confidence and improve your outcomes, Galinsky and Magee have found.</p>
<p>Regardless of its source—a strong BATNA, a powerful role, or a feeling of power—power is critical to improving your negotiated outcomes. When preparing for a negotiation with a powerful counterpart, try to increase your own sense of power on as many of these levels as possible.<br />

## 中文译文

根据西北大学教授亚当·D·加林斯基（Adam D. Galinsky）与纽约大学教授乔·C·马吉（Joe C. Magee）的研究，谈判中的权力通常源于以下三种来源之一。

谈判权力——三大主要来源  

1. 强大的BATNA（最佳替代方案）。你的“达成协议的最佳替代方案”（Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement，简称BATNA），往往是你最有力的议价资本。通过培育一个强有力的外部备选方案，你便拥有了拒绝不利交易、潇洒离场的实力。例如，一位购房者若能找到另一套同样令她满意的房子，就能在与卖方的谈判中显著提升自身议价能力。

2. 角色权力。权力也可能源自显赫的角色、头衔或职位，例如组织内较高的职级。举例来说，在与上司谈判时，你有时可能不得不让步于他的偏好，原因正在于他所处的高位赋予其天然权威。

3. 心理权力。谈判者还可将一种“心理上的权力感”带入谈判桌——即主观上感受到自己拥有力量，无论这种感受是否客观成立。加林斯基与马吉发现，仅需回想自己人生中曾手握实权的某段经历，就足以增强自信，并切实改善谈判结果。

无论权力来自强大的BATNA、显赫的职位，抑或内在的心理力量，它对于提升谈判成效都至关重要。当你即将与一位强势对手展开谈判时，应尽可能从上述多个层面入手，主动强化自身的权力感。
