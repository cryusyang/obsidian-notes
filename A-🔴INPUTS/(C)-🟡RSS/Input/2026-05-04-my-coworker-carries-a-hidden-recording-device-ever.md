<p>A reader writes:</p>
<blockquote><p>I work in higher education, in an area that is particularly under political fire. Due to anti-DEIA legislation, there have been people who have been targeted and fired due to anti-diversity advocacy. Some of the incidents have involved video that had been taken clandestinely and then edited for maximum damage. This has led to people losing their jobs and created a space of paranoia.</p>
<p>I work in an environment that requires collaboration and collegiality in order to complete work. During a casual meeting with a friendly colleague, they mentioned that another colleague showed them a piece of tech that they were now carrying that allowed them to record the people around them without their knowledge. Think Meta glasses but actually more discreet (like an AI transcribing device you can carry in your pocket). This information was *kind of* given in confidence, as the person who told me was the only one would know that our colleague was walking around with it. I hope to circle back to have a deeper conversation about what could be shared once I get your advice.</p>
<p>I walked away from that conversation kind of freaked out. My profession has specific norms around privacy that are definitely in contrast to this technology and our front-facing policies reflect those norms. But our policy norms are not the same as the larger workplace and there are definitely a small but loud minority of people who would try to argue for the use of the tech.</p>
<p>Regardless, I am extremely uncomfortable with the idea of this colleague wandering from meeting to meeting, recording coworkers without their knowledge. The space I work is intensely hierarchical and while I’m not at the bottom of the hierarchy, I don’t actually interact with this person. So I technically don’t have a way to directly make him stop. But I do have strong networks in administration that I could involve. This also brings larger issues about recording colleagues, trust in the workplace and current standards of privacy.</p>
<p>I guess I’m asking, am I overthinking/overreacting? And if I’m not, what should be the next step and what recommendations can I make to try to make sure that my colleagues are aware that we have a recorder in our midst?</p></blockquote>
<p>You are not overthinking or overreacting. Most workplaces have policies or practices that assume or require that people be informed before they’re recorded, and having someone surreptitiously recording every work conversation they’re involved in (and then having the data sent elsewhere to be processed and stored by AI) raises enormous security issues.</p>
<p>As these devices get more common, employers are going to need to come up with more explicit policies to address their use.</p>
<p>In fact, are you sure that your organization <i>doesn’t</i> have existing policies that would cover this? It’s possible that they do, even if those policies didn’t envision this specific technology.</p>
<p>Either way, though, this is a very, very reasonable thing to raise. In fact, I’d argue that now that you know about it, you have an <em>obligation</em> to raise it (doubly so if you’re in any kind of leadership or senior role). Go to those strong administration networks you mentioned, explain what you’ve become aware of, share your concerns, and ask how to address it.</p>

一位读者来信写道：

我在高等教育领域工作，所在部门正面临尤为尖锐的政治压力。受反DEIA（多元、公平、包容与归属感）立法影响，已有人因公开反对多样性而遭到针对性打击并被解雇。其中一些事件涉及秘密拍摄的视频，随后经过刻意剪辑以造成最大伤害。这不仅导致相关人员失业，更在校园内催生了一种普遍的焦虑与猜疑氛围。

我的工作环境高度依赖协作与同事间的相互尊重，方能顺利完成各项任务。一次与一位关系友善的同事进行非正式交流时，对方提到：另一位同事向其展示了一款新设备，此人如今随身携带，可悄无声息地录制周围人员的言行——类似Meta智能眼镜，但实际更为隐蔽（例如一款可置于口袋中的AI语音转录设备）。这一信息虽属半私密性质，因为只有告知我的这位同事知晓该同事正随身携带此设备，但我也因此成为除当事人外唯一知情者。待收到您的建议后，我希望能就此展开更深入的探讨：究竟哪些信息可以、又应当在组织内部共享？

那次谈话结束后，我内心颇感震惊与不安。我所从事的职业对隐私保护有着明确且严格的规范，而这类技术显然与此类职业伦理背道而驰；我们面向公众的各项政策也明确体现了这些规范。然而，这些专业层面的政策规范，并不等同于整个职场通行的实践标准；现实中确有一小部分人虽属少数，却立场鲜明、声音响亮，试图为这类技术的使用辩护。

无论如何，我极度反感这样一位同事频繁出入各类会议，在未经任何同事同意的情况下持续录音。我所处的工作环境等级森严；尽管我并非处于权力结构最底层，但我与这位同事并无直接工作交集，因此从制度上讲，我确实无法直接要求其停止此类行为。不过，我在校方管理层中拥有稳固的人际网络，可以借助这些渠道介入此事。这件事同时也牵涉更深层次的议题：同事间相互录音的边界何在？职场信任如何维系？当下隐私保护的标准又应如何界定？

我想请教的是：我是否反应过度、思虑过甚？倘若并非如此，那么下一步我该怎么做？我又该如何提出切实可行的建议，确保同事们意识到——我们中间正有一位随身携带录音设备的人？
