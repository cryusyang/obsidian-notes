<p>I&#8217;m delighted to announce that we just opened registrations for <strong>Open</strong> – a new 3-day, in-person experience happening April 28–30, 2026 in Las Vegas.</p>



<p>Yes, this month – just two weeks from now.</p>



<p>Open isn’t a typical workshop or seminar. It’s an inner-directed, experiential space designed to help you explore and harmonize your relationship with yourself… and with Life.</p>



<p>We’ll be working with reflection, gentle movement, emotional awareness, and shared presence – creating a space that’s warm, real, and deeply human.</p>



<p>The focus is on opening to more clarity, more connection, more freedom and flow, and a more harmonious way of living.</p>



<p>This will be a relatively small, in-person gathering – maximum capacity of about 150 people. We won&#8217;t be recording or streaming it since we&#8217;re prioritizing intimacy.</p>

## 中文译文

我很高兴地宣布，我们刚刚开放了“Open”活动的报名注册——这是一场为期三天、线下举办的全新体验活动，将于2026年4月28日至30日在拉斯维加斯举行。

没错，就在本月——仅两周之后。

“Open”并非一场常规的工作坊或研讨会。它是一个由内而生、注重亲身体验的空间，旨在帮助你探索并调和你与自身的关系……以及你与生命本身的关系。

我们将通过静心反思、轻柔律动、情绪觉察与共同临在等方式展开实践，营造出一个温暖、真实且充满深度人性化的空间。

活动的核心，在于助你打开自己，从而收获更清晰的觉知、更深层的联结、更丰沛的自由与流动感，以及一种更为和谐的生活方式。

这将是一场规模相对较小的线下聚会，人数上限约为150人。我们不会进行录像或直播，因为我们把亲密感置于首位。
