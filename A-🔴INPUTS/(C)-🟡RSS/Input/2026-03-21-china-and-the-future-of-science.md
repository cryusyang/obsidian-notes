<b>THE CHINESE</b> socio-political system differs from our own. From the perspective of the topic of this conference, here is the most salient distinction: the Chinese system has a telos. The Chinese party-state is fundamentally a set of goal-oriented institutions. This is not unique to China—it is in fact a distinguishing feature of all Leninist systems. I sometimes think of Leninist systems as a little bit like that bus in the movie Speed. Who here has seen it? For those who haven't, here is basic gist of that film: an extortionist attaches a bomb to the speedometer of a bus. If the bus ever slows below 50 miles per hour, everyone blows up. So it is with your average communist system. Either it hurtles towards some clearly defined goal or things start to fall apart.

## 中文译文

中国的社会政治体制与我们的体制不同。就本次会议的主题而言，最显著的区别在于：中国的体制具有明确的目标导向性。中国共产党领导的国家体制从根本上说是一套以目标为导向的制度体系。这并非中国所独有——事实上，它恰恰是所有列宁主义体制的标志性特征。我有时会把列宁主义体制比作电影《生死时速》（Speed）里的那辆公交车。在座各位有谁看过这部电影吗？若有人尚未看过，我来简要介绍一下剧情：一名罪犯在一辆公交车的速度表上安装了一枚炸弹，一旦车速降至每小时50英里以下，车上所有人便会一同爆炸。共产主义体制通常也是如此：它必须全速奔向某个清晰界定的目标；否则，整个系统便开始分崩离析。
