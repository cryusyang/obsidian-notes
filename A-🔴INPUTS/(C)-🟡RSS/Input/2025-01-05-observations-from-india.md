<b>In November 2024, I traveled to India</b> as part of a delegation hosted by the India Foundation. The foundation is a part of the new nationalist establishment steering Indian society. As they see things, India’s relationship with America has been mediated by hostile parties for too long. On the Indian side you have Congress-sympathizing functionaries; on the American side, a set of intellectuals and diplomats who can neither speak for nor to the American right. Direct links between Indian and American nationalists are needed.

So I was invited India.

## 中文译文

2024年11月，我作为印度基金会主办的代表团成员赴印度访问。该基金会是当前主导印度社会的新民族主义建制派的一部分。在他们看来，印美关系长期以来一直受到敌对势力的干扰：印度方面是同情国大党的官僚人士；美国方面则是一群既无法代表、也无法与美国右翼对话的知识分子和外交官。因此，印度与美国民族主义者之间亟需建立直接联系。

于是，我受邀前往印度。
