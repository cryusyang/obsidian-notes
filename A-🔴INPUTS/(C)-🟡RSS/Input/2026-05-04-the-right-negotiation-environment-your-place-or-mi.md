<h3>Choosing the Right Negotiation Environment</h3>
<p><strong> Negotiating at your place. </strong>Like athletes seeking the home-field advantage, most people prefer to negotiate on their own territory. Meeting at your office offers many perceived benefits. First, you gain the advantage of familiarity with the negotiating environment. You know where everything is located, from reliable secretarial services to secure areas for private consultation. Your counterpart, on the other hand, runs the risk of unfamiliarity.</p>
<p>Negotiating at home also allows you to control the negotiation environment, including the selection and arrangement of the meeting room, the seating of participants at the <a href="https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/bargaining-table/">bargaining table</a>, and the nature and timing of hospitality and social events. Playing host gives you the opportunity to impress the other team with your company&#8217;s resources. Finally, negotiating at home is cheaper, eliminating travel costs and saving executive time.</p>
<p>Whereas host negotiators can usually continue to handle other job demands while participating in talks, a visiting negotiator cannot do the same. Negotiating at home also spares you the pressures of being away from your family, friends, and daily routines. Visiting executives may tend to make a deal or break off talks more quickly than if they were negotiating on their own turf-often to their disadvantage.</p>
<p><strong> Negotiating at your counterpart&#8217;s place. </strong>At first blush, negotiating on your counterpart&#8217;s turf seems to offer only disadvantages: travel can be costly; the negotiation environment is unfamiliar and uncontrollable; lines of communication to your home office may be slow, uncertain, and insecure; and, when traveling a great distance, you face the psychological pressures of being away from home.</p>
<p>On the other hand, when you&#8217;re the seller, often the only way to bring your product or service to the other side&#8217;s attention is to show up at his office. The choice of a negotiating site also has symbolic value. By entering your counterpart&#8217;s territory, you show your serious intent and strong desire to make a deal-factors that can be invaluable in persuading him to sign a contract.</p>
<p>The most important reason to negotiate on the other side&#8217;s territory is that it gives you opportunities to learn. When making deals, executives are laying the foundation for a continuing relationship-which depends crucially on how well parties know each other. A vital purpose of any <a href="https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/business-negotiation/">business negotiation</a> is to allow both sides to learn about each other, their businesses, and the conditions in which they must operate. In this respect, the home field <em>doesn&#8217;t </em>hold an advantage.</p>

## 中文译文

人人都知道房地产行业的三大法则：“地段！地段！地段！”而在促成交易的过程中，选择恰当的谈判环境同样至关重要。你所选定的场所，不仅会影响讨论的基调、左右谈判进程，更最终关系到协议结果的成败。“在你那儿谈，还是在我这儿谈？”这一看似简单的问题，实则需要审慎权衡即将展开的谈判本身，以及你希望营造的互动格局。

如何选择最合适的谈判环境？

在己方所在地谈判  
正如运动员力求主场优势，多数人也倾向于在自己的地盘上展开谈判。在自家办公室会面，往往被认为具有多重优势。首先，您对谈判环境了如指掌——从可靠的行政支持服务，到可供私密磋商的安全区域，一切尽在掌握；而对方却可能因环境陌生而处于不利地位。

在本地谈判还意味着您能全面掌控谈判环境：包括会议室的选择与布置、谈判桌旁各方人员的座次安排，以及招待活动与社交环节的形式与时间安排。作为东道主，您还有机会向对方团队展示贵司的实力与资源。此外，在本地谈判成本更低——无需差旅开支，亦可节省高管宝贵的时间。

通常情况下，作为东道主的谈判者可在参与谈判的同时兼顾其他工作职责；而远道而来的谈判者则难以做到这一点。在本地谈判还能让您免受离家所带来的压力——不必远离家人、朋友及日常起居节奏。相较而言，出差前来的高管往往更急于达成协议或提前中止谈判，而这常常对其自身不利。

在对方所在地谈判  
乍看之下，在对方的地盘上谈判似乎只有劣势：差旅费用高昂；谈判环境陌生且难以掌控；与总部之间的沟通渠道可能迟滞、不可靠甚至不安全；若长途跋涉，还需承受远离家乡带来的心理压力。

然而，若您是卖方，有时唯有亲赴对方办公室，才能真正引起对方对您产品或服务的关注。谈判地点的选择本身亦具象征意义：主动踏入对方领地，正彰显出您严肃认真的合作态度与达成交易的强烈意愿——这些因素在说服对方签署合同的过程中，往往具有不可估量的价值。

选择在对方所在地谈判，最重要的一点在于它为您提供了学习的机会。商业交易不仅是单次协议的达成，更是长期合作关系的奠基过程；而这种关系能否稳固发展，关键取决于双方彼此了解的程度。任何商务谈判的一项核心目的，正是让双方深入了解彼此、各自的企业运作方式，以及各自所处的经营环境。就此而言，“主场优势”并不成立。
