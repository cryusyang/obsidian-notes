<b>THERE ARE DECADES WHEN</b> possibility is constrained in a narrow frame. The terrain has been surveyed, boundaries have been laid, and rules have been established. In such an age there is still room for high drama: The decisive round of a boxing match draws the eye despite the fact—or perhaps because—the boxers play an antique game. In such times and climes, victory means mastery of existing modes, not the invention of new ones.

But nothing human is everlasting. Always there comes a day when spectators search for better games and settlers seek out fresher pastures. That day of change arrives with much confusion and fanfare. Sons dishonor their fathers. Daughters rise against their mothers. Ancestral ideals are cast aside, and possibility staggers forth from its long captivity, ready to wreak vengeance on mankind.

## 中文译文

有些年代，可能性被束缚在狭小的框架之中。地形早已勘测完毕，边界业已划定，规则也已确立。在这样的时代，依然不乏扣人心弦的高潮戏码：拳击比赛的决胜回合仍能牢牢吸引人们的目光，这或许正因其选手所从事的乃是一项古老而陈旧的运动——抑或恰恰正因如此。在这样的时代与环境中，“胜利”意味着对既有模式的娴熟驾驭，而非新范式的开创。

但凡属人者，皆无永恒。总有一天，观众会渴望更富新意的比赛，拓荒者会追寻更为丰饶的牧场。而这一变革之日的到来，往往伴随着巨大的混乱与喧嚣。儿子背弃父亲，女儿反抗母亲；祖先奉行的理想被弃如敝履，而可能性则踉跄着挣脱长久以来的禁锢，准备向人类展开报复。
