<p>How did our society decide how much to count things like education and artistic taste when evaluating prestige and status? How did we pick key moral norms and values, such as democracy, gender equality, legal due process, rules of just war, and norms of good parenting? Yes, such choices are weakly influenced by our DNA, and also by cultural evolution selection pressures on individuals. But mostly these things came from cultural evolution of groups.</p><p>You may have heard that such *group selection* never happens, but that&#8217;s wrong. Not only do most cultural evolution scholars see group selection as a key force, group selection also seems to important in DNA evolution, where species are groups. The fact that more species today descended from fragmented habits like rivers, coral reefs, and rainforests, where habitats were smaller, suggests that group selection of species has actually mattered more for DNA than individual selection within species.</p><p>I&#8217;ve said previously that healthy cultural evolution for stuff like norms status markers depends on four key parameters: enough cultural variety, strong enough selection pressures on cultures, slow enough internal cultural drift, and slow enough rates of environmental change. But I have to admit that this first &#8220;variety&#8221; parameter is a sloppy way to talk about it. Counting the number of cultures would make sense if, as with species for DNA, there was only one clear scale at which people are joined into cultural groups. But in fact cultural behaviors cluster together at many different scales.</p><p>However, I&#8217;ve been doing some reading, and have found that for decades cultural evolution scholars have had a less-sloppy substitute concept: &#8220;network structure&#8221;. If you look at the details of who people interact with, and who they are likely to copy their behaviors from, the shape of the network of such ties matters a lot for cultural group selection.</p><p>For example, the network feature that most promotes group selection seems to be &#8220;modularity&#8221;, roughly how many more ties there are within clusters, compared to between clusters. It also matters how similar are people within clusters, how much overlap there is between interaction and emulation networks, how well prestige tracks adaptiveness, how much conformity pressure there is for a behavior, and how much that behavior effects visible outcomes that people care about.</p><p>Each different type of behavior can have its own different network, and its own different coordination scale, requiring group selection at that cluster scale or above in order to select adaptive versions of that behavior. But it seems clear that relevant scales for many kinds of behaviors have greatly increased over the last few centuries, greatly reducing the effective &#8220;variety&#8221; for the purposes of cultural evolution. And this is plausibly cutting the effect strength of group selection, likely enough to cause net maladaptive change to our norms and status markers.</p>

## 中文译文

我们的社会在评估声望与地位时，是如何确定教育程度、艺术品味等要素的权重的？我们又是如何选定诸如民主、性别平等、法律正当程序、正义战争准则以及良好育儿规范等核心道德准则与价值观的？诚然，此类选择在一定程度上受到我们基因（DNA）的影响，也受到作用于个体身上的文化演化选择压力的影响；但总体而言，这些准则与价值观主要源于群体层面的文化演化。

你或许曾听人断言：所谓“群体选择”根本不会发生——但这种说法是错误的。不仅大多数文化演化学者都将群体选择视为一种关键驱动力，而且在基于DNA的生物演化中，群体选择似乎同样重要；毕竟，物种本身即是一种群体。如今，更多现存物种源自河流、珊瑚礁和热带雨林等栖息地破碎化的环境——这些环境中单个栖息地面积较小——这一事实表明，在DNA演化过程中，物种层面的群体选择，其重要性甚至可能超过了物种内部的个体选择。

我此前曾指出，像社会规范、地位标识这类文化现象要实现健康的文化演化，需依赖四个关键参数：足够丰富的文化多样性、足够强的文化选择压力、足够缓慢的文化内部漂变速度，以及足够缓慢的环境变化速率。但我也必须承认，其中第一个参数——“多样性”——其实是一种较为粗略的表述方式。若文化群体与生物物种类似，仅存在一个清晰明确的尺度，将人们自然划分为彼此界限分明的文化群体，那么单纯统计文化群体的数量便确有意义；然而现实中，文化行为却在诸多不同尺度上呈现出聚类现象。

不过，近期我通过阅读发现，数十年来，文化演化学者早已提出一个更为严谨的替代概念：“网络结构”。若深入考察人们实际互动的对象，以及他们更可能效仿哪些人的行为，便可发现，这种人际联结所构成的网络形态，对文化群体选择过程具有至关重要的影响。

例如，最能促进群体选择的网络特征似乎是“模块性”（modularity），即集群内部联结数量相较于集群之间联结数量高出多少。此外，集群内部成员间的相似程度、互动网络与模仿网络之间的重合度、声望能否准确反映适应性、某种行为所承受的从众压力大小，以及该行为是否显著影响人们所关切的可见结果——这些因素也都至关重要。

不同类型的行为，往往各自拥有不同的网络结构与协调尺度；要筛选出适应性更强的行为版本，就必须在相应集群尺度或更高尺度上展开群体选择。然而显而易见的是，近几个世纪以来，许多类型行为所对应的关联尺度已大幅扩展，从而极大削弱了文化演化意义上有效的“多样性”。这种趋势很可能正在削弱群体选择的作用强度，甚至足以导致我们的社会规范与地位标识整体上发生适应不良的变迁。
