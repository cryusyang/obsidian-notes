<p>A reader writes:</p>
<blockquote><p>I own a small takeout restaurant. We have four employees, plus me and my business partner. It’s hard to hire and when we find employees who show up every day and meet our expectations, we try to keep them happy.</p>
<p>We’ve had one employee for about 2.5 years now. Slowly over time, she has started taking more and more liberties in regards to food and ignoring our requests for her to do a task. I’ve had conversations with her three times, and things always get better for a period and then she starts to slip again.</p>
<p>Recently, she’s started taking more than the $10 meal we provide per day (covers a sandwich, side, and drink). She’ll take an extra drink once or twice a month, or today she’d taken her free meal on her break and then I returned from an errand to find her eating a bag of chips while she was supposed to be working.</p>
<p>This all feels so petty. How do I have a conversation about $1.50? But, it makes me batty that I’ve had to talk to her about it more than once, and that it just keeps happening. When she’s taking more, it’s a couple dollars here or there. But over time it adds up. Our margins are tight, and our costs have skyrocketed this past year.</p>
<p>A part of me thinks if we’re too strict on these things, we’ll lose employees and hiring is one of the biggest challenges we face. But I also feel like she’s stealing from us and my ego just wants to scream. Should I address it again, or just find a way to let it go and accept that it is what it is?</p></blockquote>

## 中文译文

一位读者来信写道：

我经营一家小型外卖餐厅，共有四名员工，外加我和我的生意合伙人。招聘员工非常困难，因此，一旦我们找到那些每天准时上班、且能符合我们期望的员工，我们就会尽力让他们感到满意。

其中一名员工已在我们这里工作约两年半了。但随着时间推移，她渐渐开始在餐饮方面越来越随意，也屡次无视我们安排给她的任务。我已就此与她谈过三次，每次谈话后情况都会暂时好转，可不久之后她又故态复萌。

最近，她开始突破我们每日提供的10美元员工餐标准（含一份三明治、一份小食和一杯饮料）。她每月会多拿一两次饮料；而就在今天，她已在休息时间领用了免费员工餐，我办完事回来时，却发现她正在工作时间吃一包薯片。

这一切听起来实在微不足道——我该如何就区区1.5美元与她展开对话？可更让我恼火的是，我已经为此反复提醒过她多次，问题却始终得不到根治。虽然每次多拿的东西只值几美元，但日积月累，数额也不容忽视。我们的利润空间本就十分微薄，而过去一年各项成本更是大幅飙升。

我内心一部分声音提醒我：若在这些小事上过于严苛，恐怕会失去员工；而招人难，恰恰是我们当前面临的最大挑战之一。但另一部分声音却强烈认为，她这种行为无异于从我们这里偷窃，我的自尊心甚至忍不住想大声质问。我究竟该再次当面指出这个问题，还是干脆放手，接受现状、随它去呢？
