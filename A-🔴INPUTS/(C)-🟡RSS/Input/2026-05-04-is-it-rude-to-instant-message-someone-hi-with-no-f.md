<p>A reader writes:</p>
<blockquote><p>At my company, we have an instant messaging system. A lot of people will send an initial message that says nothing but “you free?” or “hi.”</p>
<p>In addition to making me irrationally annoyed (just tell me what you want already!), I have no idea what the appropriate response is. Is it “yes,” “hello Bob,” “what’s up”? All of these seem terrible.</p>
<p>What is appropriate IM protocol? I like to start with, “Do you have time for a question about X?” Or just the question if it’s short because that’s what I’d prefer to receive, but maybe people find this rude? I am aware that I am overthinking this but I also can’t stop overthinking it.</p></blockquote>

## 中文译文

一位读者来信写道：

我们公司使用即时通讯系统。很多人发来的第一条消息往往只有“你有空吗？”或“嗨”这样的内容。这不仅让我莫名烦躁（干脆直接告诉我你想干什么不就好了！），我还真不知道该怎么得体地回复才好。回个“有空”，还是“你好，鲍勃”，抑或“最近怎么样”？这些回应似乎都不太合适。

那么，即时通讯的得体礼仪究竟是什么？我个人习惯以“你方便问一个关于X的问题吗？”开头；如果问题很简短，我甚至会直接抛出问题本身——毕竟这是我希望收到的消息形式。但这样会不会显得失礼？我清楚自己是在过度纠结这件事，可偏偏又停不下来。
