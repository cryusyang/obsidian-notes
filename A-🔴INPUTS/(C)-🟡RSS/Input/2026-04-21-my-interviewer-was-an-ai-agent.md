> 一位被裁员的求职者在应聘心仪职位时，遭遇了强制性的AI面试初筛，过程体验极差：问题模糊无关、频繁打断回答、三次断线重录，且全程录像导致回答显得疲惫生硬。作者质疑这种单向、技术故障频发的筛选方式违背了雇主与求职者间的基本信任契约——即简历通过后应有双向沟通机会，以便双方评估匹配度。文章指出，要求求职者在未获任何反馈前就投入大量时间完成AI测试、视频面试或模拟任务，本质上是脱离实际、损害候选人体验的招聘实践。

---

<p>A reader writes:</p>
<blockquote><p>After being laid off, I was aggressively applying to everything even remotely in my industry. I landed an interview with a company I recognized and a role I was fully qualified for. In order to move forward in the process, however, they said I needed to &#8220;complete an AI screening.&#8221;</p>
<p>What? I was expecting a phone call with the hiring manager as a first step, but this is the future I guess. So I went with it. Well, it was — perhaps predictably — absolutely awful. Not only did the AI ask me confusing, irrelevant questions about hyper specific bullet points on my resume, but it frequently interrupted my responses and even lost connection three times, forcing me to repeat myself. All this happened while I was being video recorded, so I&#8217;m sure some of my answers likely came off a bit clipped and tired.</p>
<p>I have no idea how I should have handled that, and I dread having to do it again for some other company. Do you have any advice?</p></blockquote>
<p>Yeah, it’s a terrible practice for exactly the reasons you encountered: nonsensical questions, technical issues, and the expectation that you’ll invest your time in a call to answer questions without getting any of your own questions answered in return so you can determine if you’re even interested in moving forward.</p>
<p>Up until now, the social contract between employers and job-seekers has been that once your resume passes an initial screening, the next step (whether it’s a phone screen or a more in-depth interview) will give you the chance to <i>talk</i> to the employer to ask questions of your own so that you can figure out if you want to invest any more of your time afterwards, since it doesn’t make sense to do that if the role doesn’t fit what you’re looking for. That’s why employer demands like <a href="https://www.askamanager.org/2018/02/im-supposed-to-write-23-mini-essays-in-24-hours-before-an-employer-will-even-interview-me.html">“write multiple essays before we’ll interview you&#8221;</a> or <a href="https://www.askamanager.org/2022/05/jobs-that-require-a-time-consuming-exercise-before-you-can-even-apply.html">“do a lengthy work simulation before you can even apply&#8221;</a> have always seemed out-of-touch.</p>
<p>It was bad enough when some employers started requiring <a href="https://www.askamanager.org/2025/03/whats-the-deal-with-one-way-recorded-video-interviews.html">one-way video interviews</a> before they’d talk to you (where you have to record yourself answering specific questions, again without the opportunity to ask your own in return). This is that taken to new heights, and with even less respect for you and your time.</p>
<p>But AI interviews are probably sticking around for at least a while, if not long-term, so job-seekers will have to figure out whether they’re willing to play along or not. If you have other options, you can always decline. But like most terrible hiring practices, this one will most affect the people with the fewest options. If you’re at a certain level in your field and have in-demand skills, you can say, “I’d be happy to talk with a human who can answer my questions in return; if that’s not possible, I’ll need to withdraw from consideration.” If you’re not in that position and just need a job, you probably need to roll your eyes and do it.</p>

## 中文译文

一位读者来信写道：

被裁员后，我积极投递简历，只要是与我所在行业稍有关联的职位都不放过。后来，我获得了一家知名公司的面试机会，应聘的岗位我也完全符合要求。然而，对方却告知我，要想进入下一环节，必须“完成一项人工智能（AI）初筛”。

什么？我原本以为第一步会是与招聘经理通个电话，但看来这就是未来吧。于是，我还是照做了。结果——或许不出所料——整个过程糟糕透顶。AI不仅向我提出大量令人困惑、毫不相关的问题，还紧盯我简历上那些极其琐碎的要点不放；更糟的是，它频频打断我的回答，甚至三次中断连接，迫使我反复作答。而这一切都发生在视频录制过程中，因此我确信，自己的部分回答听起来一定显得生硬、疲惫、缺乏连贯性。

我完全不知道当时该如何应对，一想到今后可能还要为其他公司再经历一次这样的流程，就倍感恐惧。您有什么建议吗？

是的，这种做法的确糟糕至极，原因恰恰就是您所遭遇的：问题毫无逻辑、技术故障频发，且单方面要求你投入时间作答，却得不到任何反馈或回应——你甚至无法借此判断自己是否真有兴趣继续推进。

迄今为止，雇主与求职者之间一直存在一种不成文的约定：一旦你的简历通过初步筛选，下一步（无论是电话初筛还是更深入的面试）就应当为你提供与雇主直接沟通的机会，让你也能提出自己的问题，从而判断这份工作是否真的契合你的期待与需求。毕竟，若岗位本身并不符合你的职业目标，再投入更多时间便毫无意义。正因如此，“在安排面试前先撰写多篇长文”或“未提交申请前就需完成冗长的工作模拟任务”这类雇主要求，历来被视为脱离现实、缺乏同理心。

此前，已有部分雇主开始强制要求求职者在正式接触前完成单向视频面试（即你需自行录制对特定问题的回答，且依然无法反向提问），这已属不妥；而如今的AI面试，则将这一问题推向了更荒谬的境地，也进一步漠视你的价值与时间。

但AI面试短期内恐怕难以消失，甚至可能长期存在，因此求职者不得不思考：自己是否愿意配合？如果你还有其他选择，当然可以拒绝。然而，和许多糟糕的招聘实践一样，这种机制最终伤害最深的，往往是选择最少的人。如果你在业内已具备一定资历，手握市场急需的技能，大可坦然表示：“我很乐意与真人招聘经理交流，彼此问答；若这一点无法满足，我只能遗憾退出此次应聘。”但如果你尚不具备这样的议价能力，只是迫切需要一份工作，那恐怕也只能翻个白眼，咬牙完成了。
