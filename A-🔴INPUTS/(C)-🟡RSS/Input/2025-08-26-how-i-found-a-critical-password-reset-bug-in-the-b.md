<p>As a bug bounty hunter, I often test mobile apps by routing traffic through <a href="https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/desktop/mobile/config-android-device">Burp Suite</a> to see how the backend APIs behave. One of my recent hunts led me to an interesting endpoint in the <strong>BB Parents app</strong> that turned into a serious vulnerability.</p><h4>Finding the Endpoint:</h4><p>I extracted the endpoints from the <strong>JavaScript files</strong> and built a custom wordlist.<br /> Then I used <strong>FFUF</strong> with that wordlist to perform fuzzing.</p><ol><li>While testing the Android app, I noticed a request to this endpoint: <a href="https://www.securly.com/parents/application/v4/admin">https://www.redact.com/parents/application/v4/admin</a></li><li>Naturally, I decided to <strong>fuzz the endpoint</strong>. By appending random words and observing responses, I eventually discovered: <a href="https://www.securly.com/parents/application/v4/admin/doRegistrationEntries">https://www.redact.com/parents/application/v4/admin/doRegistrationEntries</a></li></ol><figure><img alt="" src="https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*wQ-spG1yogujAsqBumk_hQ.png" /></figure><ol><li>At first glance, it looked like an endpoint for <strong>new user registration</strong>. I confirm that this is for new registration.</li></ol><h3>The Bug:</h3><p>Here’s where things got interesting.<br /> This endpoint didn’t just allow new account creation — it also let me overwrite the password of <strong>any existing account</strong> without verifying if the email was already registered.</p><p>In simple terms:</p><ul><li>If a user already had an account, sending their email + a new password to this endpoint would reset their password.</li><li>Their existing data and profile remained untouched; only the password changed.</li></ul><p>That meant I could take over any parent’s account just by knowing their email address.</p><h4>Reproducing the Issue</h4><p>The reproduction steps were very straightforward:</p><ol><li>Intercept a request from the mobile app in Burp Suite and get the endpoint.</li><li>FUZZ THE endpoint `<a href="https://www.securly.com/parents/application/v4/admin/FUZZ`">https://www.redact.com/parents/application/v4/admin/FUZZ`</a></li><li>Modify the endpoint to: `<a href="https://www.securly.com/parents/application/v4/admin/FUZZ`">https://www.redact.com</a>/parents/application/v4/admin/doRegistrationEntries`</li><li>When I hit the Send Button, Response said to Only POST request is allowed.</li><li>I switched to the post method and changed the header, Content-Type: application/json and got the are asking for two required parameters.</li></ol><figure><img alt="" src="https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*JIsuR3FEn60ZWA-icE6VtA.png" /></figure><p>6. Replace the email field with the victim’s email, and set any new password.</p><pre>{&quot;email&quot;:&quot;victim@example.com&quot;,&quot;password&quot;:&quot;New@12345&quot;}</pre><figure><img alt="" src="https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*YFDBmJNGrBKzj78fqtRBxA.png" /></figure><p>7. The response came back with `success: 1`, and now I could log in to the victim’s account.</p><h4>Impact</h4><p>This was a <strong>critical authentication bypass</strong>.<br /> Any parent’s account could be hijacked with just their email address.</p><p>Considering that the Program is used for student safety, parent communication, and school monitoring, the impact was <strong>very high</strong>.</p><h4>Reporting &amp; Reward</h4><p>I reported the bug responsibly through the H1 BB program.<br /> The team at Redact was very responsive, acknowledged the issue quickly, and fixed it.</p><p>As a token of appreciation, I received a <strong>$4,000 reward</strong> for this finding.</p><h4>Key Takeaways</h4><blockquote><em>Always fuzz API endpoints — hidden functions often reveal themselves this way.</em></blockquote><blockquote><em>Registration endpoints can sometimes double as password reset mechanisms.</em></blockquote><blockquote><em>Simple parameter manipulation (like email + password) can lead to critical account takeover issues.</em></blockquote><blockquote><em>Always report responsibly so that users stay protected.</em></blockquote><p><em>Happy Hunting</em></p><p><em>— </em><a href="https://medium.com/u/6bb88d45faec"><em>Imran Hossain</em></a></p><p><a href="https://x.com/s41n1k"><em>https://x.com/s41n1k</em></a></p><p>#bug-bounty #write-up</p><img alt="" height="1" src="https://medium.com/_/stat?event=post.clientViewed&amp;referrerSource=full_rss&amp;postId=a22fffe285e1" width="1" />
