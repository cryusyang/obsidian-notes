<b>NEITHER THE MALE AUTHORS NOR THE MALE READERS</b> most preoccupied with middle age are inclined to face it cleanly. The male author depicts the mid-life crisis to escape his own. His novels and screenplays are an adolescent retort to the anxieties of ease. Thus the implausible sexscapades, couture bloodletting, and whiny retreats into solipsism that have defined so much of American literature over the last seven decades. In this tradition, there is little difference between a literary and  a demeaning depiction of the middle class man. 

John Williams' 1965 novel Stoner is different.

## 中文译文

既非男性作者，亦非男性读者，  
那些最关注中年阶段的人，往往不愿坦然直面它。男性作家描写中年危机，实为逃避自身所面临的危机；其小说与剧本，不过是用青春期式的反应来应对安逸生活所带来的焦虑。因此，过去七十年间，大量美国文学作品充斥着不合情理的风流韵事、浮华空洞的自我剖白，以及充满怨艾的唯我论式退缩。在此传统之下，对中产阶级男性的文学化呈现与贬抑性刻画之间，几乎毫无区别。  

而约翰·威廉姆斯一九六五年出版的小说《斯通纳》，却迥然不同。
