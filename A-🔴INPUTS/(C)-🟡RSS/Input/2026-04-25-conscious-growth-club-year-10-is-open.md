<p><a href="https://stevepavlina.com/conscious-growth-club/"><strong>Conscious Growth Club Year 10</strong></a> is now open for enrollment.</p>



<p>CGC is our private, growth-oriented community for people who want more honesty, courage, support, connection, and meaningful forward movement in their lives – not as some kind of self-improvement grind, but as a richer way to live.</p>



<p>We’re beginning our 10th CGC year on May 1st, and you’re warmly invited to join us.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://stevepavlina.com/conscious-growth-club/"><img alt="CGC Year 10 Opening" class="wp-image-27189" height="576" src="https://stevepavlina.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/CGC-Year-10-Opening-1024x576.jpg" width="1024" /></a></figure>



<p>If you’ve been craving a more grounded and supportive space for your personal growth journey, this may be a very good year to come in.</p>



<p>CGC is especially for people who want to keep growing, but who don’t want to do it alone or only through passive learning. It’s a live, interactive, human space where members bring real life into the room – goals, transitions, stuck points, creative projects, relationship challenges, lifestyle shifts, health intentions, business questions, emotional patterns, and those strange “I know something needs to change, but I don’t know what yet” moments.</p>



<p>That kind of growth is much easier when you have a room of thoughtful, caring, self-aware people to explore with.</p>



<p>A lot of people try to navigate their growth with too much isolation. They read books, watch videos, journal, think, plan, and chat with AI now and then. Those can all be useful, but at some point the inner work often needs a real human relational space to deepen and advance.</p>



<p>It helps to be seen and acknowledged by like-hearted people.</p>



<p>It helps to hear how other people are navigating similar challenges.</p>



<p>It helps to share what’s really going on without having to polish it first.</p>



<p>It helps to be welcomed and received as a vibrant member of a rich social community.</p>



<p>It helps to be in a space where growth is normal – where people aren’t shocked that you’re rethinking your work, your relationships, your habits, your identity, your emotional patterns, your lifestyle, or your whole relationship with life.</p>



<p>That’s a big part of what CGC provides. What&#8217;s unusual in the outside world – conscious growth – is totally normal in our space.</p>



<p>CGC is not a therapy group. It’s not a social media group. It’s not a pile of static content. It’s a live community and growth environment with a strong emphasis on participation, connection, honesty, and practical support.</p>



<p>During Year 10, we’ll have several dozen live Zoom calls together, mostly around 90 minutes each. These calls are designed to help members reflect, connect, get unstuck, clarify intentions, share progress, explore meaningful questions, and keep translating insight into real-life movement.</p>



<p>We’ll also continue using our private member forums and CGC portal, so you can stay connected between calls, access courses and resources, and participate at your own pace.</p>



<p>What I especially love about CGC is the range of what members bring into the space. One person may be navigating a career transition. Another may be working on health or fitness. Someone else may be deepening their relationship life, exploring a creative project, or trying to bring more courage and truth into their daily decisions.</p>



<p>The common thread is not that everyone has the same goals.</p>



<p>The common thread is willingness.</p>



<p>Willingness to tell the truth.</p>



<p>Willingness to keep growing.</p>



<p>Willingness to participate.</p>



<p>Willingness to be seen.</p>



<p>Willingness to support others while also receiving support.</p>



<p>That makes a big difference.</p>



<p>CGC tends to work best for people who already have some self-development experience and who are ready for a more interactive, higher-trust environment. You don’t need to be perfect, polished, or constantly productive. You don’t need to have your whole life figured out. But you do need to be willing to show up honestly and engage with the room.</p>



<p>This year we’re making CGC feel even more practical, relational, and alive – with more emphasis on translating meaningful intentions into daily life. More real support. More personalized engagement. More warmth. More meaningful connection. More growth that actually impacts daily life and results.</p>



<p>If that sounds good to you, please take a look at the invitation page here:</p>



<p><a href="https://stevepavlina.com/conscious-growth-club/"><strong>Join Conscious Growth Club Year 10</strong></a></p>



<p>Enrollment is open now and closes May 7th at 11:59 PM Pacific time.</p>



<p>CGC Year 10 officially begins May 1st and runs through April 30, 2027. When you join, you’ll get access right away, so you can begin exploring the member portal and community space before the new CGC year begins.</p>



<p>The price is $3333 for new members and for returning past members.</p>



<p>If you’re a renewing member, your renewal price is $1111.</p>



<p>If you feel a clear yes, I’d love to welcome you into the room for Year 10.</p>



<p>And if you read the invitation page and sense that it’s not your space, that’s perfectly okay too. CGC is intentionally not for everyone. It works best when people self-select honestly. That honest self-selection has worked really well for us, helping to create a community of members who want the full, rich, consciously engaged experience.</p>



<p>If some part of you has been wanting a more alive, honest, supportive growth environment – a place where you can keep evolving with thoughtful people who actually care – then I encourage you to listen to that signal.</p>



<p>This is a beautiful year to join us.</p>

## 中文译文

“有意识成长俱乐部”第十期现已开放报名。

CGC 是一个私密的、以成长为导向的社群，面向那些渴望在生活中获得更多真诚、勇气、支持、联结，以及有意义的前行力量的人——这种成长并非某种自我提升式的苦修，而是一种更丰盈的生活方式。

我们将于 5 月 1 日开启 CGC 的第十个年份，诚挚邀请您加入我们。

如果您一直渴望一个更踏实、更具支持性的空间，来陪伴您的个人成长旅程，那么今年或许正是您加入的绝佳时机。

CGC 尤其适合这样一群人：他们希望持续成长，却不愿独自前行，也不满足于仅靠被动学习（如阅读、听课）来实现成长。这是一个真实、互动、充满人性温度的空间——会员们将真实生活带入其中：目标设定、人生转折、卡点困境、创意项目、亲密关系挑战、生活方式调整、健康目标、事业困惑、情绪模式，以及那些难以言说的时刻：“我知道有些事必须改变，却还不清楚究竟该变什么。”

当您拥有一群深思熟虑、富有同理心、具备高度自我觉察力的伙伴共同探索时，这样的成长会轻松许多。

许多人试图在过度孤立的状态下推进自己的成长：读书、看视频、写日记、独自思考、制定计划，偶尔与 AI 聊天。这些方式固然有用，但内在成长行至深处，往往需要一个真实的人际关系空间，才能真正深化与跃迁。

被志同道合者看见与确认，很有帮助；  
听到他人如何应对相似的挑战，很有帮助；  
无需事先打磨、就能坦率分享当下真实状态，很有帮助；  
作为鲜活的一员，被欣然接纳、真诚欢迎，融入一个丰富而温暖的社会性社群，很有帮助；  
身处一个“成长本就是常态”的环境，也很有帮助——在这里，人们不会因你重新思考职业、关系、习惯、身份认同、情绪模式、生活方式，甚至你与整个生命的关系而感到惊讶。

这正是 CGC 所提供的核心价值之一。在外部世界中显得另类的“有意识成长”，在我们的空间里却是再自然不过的事。

CGC 不是心理治疗小组，不是社交媒体群组，也不是一堆静态内容的堆砌。它是一个鲜活的社群与成长场域，高度重视成员的主动参与、彼此联结、坦诚表达与切实支持。

在第十期中，我们将共同举办数十场线上 Zoom 直播会议，每次约 90 分钟。这些会议旨在帮助会员反思自身、建立联结、突破卡点、厘清意图、分享进展、探讨深层问题，并持续将洞见转化为现实生活中的切实行动。

我们也将继续使用专属会员论坛与 CGC 门户平台，让您在会议间隙保持联结、随时访问课程与资源，并按自己的节奏参与其中。

我尤其喜爱 CGC 的一点，是会员们带入这个空间的生命议题之广博：有人正经历职业转型；有人专注于健康或体能提升；有人深耕亲密关系，有人投入创意项目，也有人努力让日常决策更具勇气与真实。

大家的共同点，并非拥有相同的目标，  
而在于一种共通的意愿：

愿意讲出真相；  
愿意持续成长；  
愿意积极参与；  
愿意被看见；  
愿意在支持他人的同时，也坦然接受支持。

这份意愿，意义重大。

CGC 最适合那些已有一定自我发展经验、并准备好进入一个更具互动性、更高信任度环境的人。您无需完美、无需精致、无需时刻高效产出；您不必已将人生全部理清。但您需要愿意以真实的姿态出现，并与这个空间深度互动。

今年，我们致力于让 CGC 显得更加务实、更具人际温度、也更富生命力——更强调将深刻而有意义的意图，切实落地于日常生活之中：提供更真实的支持，更个性化的互动，更温暖的氛围，更有深度的联结，以及真正能影响日常体验与实际成果的成长。

如果您对此心生共鸣，欢迎点击下方链接，查看完整邀请页面：

加入“有意识成长俱乐部”第十期

报名现已开启，截止时间为太平洋时间 5 月 7 日晚 11:59。

CGC 第十期将于 5 月 1 日正式启动，持续至 2027 年 4 月 30 日。一旦您完成报名，即可立即获得全部权限，提前浏览会员门户与社群空间，在新一期正式开始前便开启探索之旅。

新会员及曾参加过往期的老会员，费用为 3333 美元；  
如您是续费会员，则续费价格为 1111 美元。

若您内心响起清晰而肯定的声音，请让我热忱地欢迎您加入第十期的共学空间。

若您阅读完邀请页面后，感觉这里并非属于您的地方，那也完全没问题。CGC 本就无意面向所有人。它最理想的状态，是依靠成员诚实的自主选择——这种真诚的自我筛选，多年来成效卓著，帮助我们凝聚起一群真正渴望全然投入、丰盛饱满、有意识参与成长体验的同行者。

如果您内心某个角落，一直渴望一个更鲜活、更真实、更具支持性的成长环境——一个可以与真正关心您的、富有思辨力的同伴一同持续进化的所在——那么，请认真倾听这一信号。

今年，正是加入我们的美好时刻。
