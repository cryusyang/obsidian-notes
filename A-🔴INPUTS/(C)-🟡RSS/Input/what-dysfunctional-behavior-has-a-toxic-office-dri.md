It's the Thursday “ask the readers” question. A reader writes:

这是周四的“向读者提问”栏目。一位读者写道：

<blockquote>Luckily no one in my office is <a href="https://www.askamanager.org/2017/07/i-bit-my-coworker.html">biting anyone</a>, but my formerly pretty-good job has devolved into a toxic mess.</blockquote>

万幸，我办公室里没人像<a href="https://www.askamanager.org/2017/07/i-bit-my-coworker.html">咬同事</a>那样离谱，但原本还相当不错的工作，已彻底沦为一场有毒的混乱。

<blockquote>I found myself pressing my ear against my wall to try and glean basic (not sensitive or confidential) information I needed to do my job by eavesdropping on a conversation next door. My officemate wasn't ruffled; instead he grabbed a glass to better hear it, because that was a reasonable reaction to the situation we are in.</blockquote>

我发现自己竟把耳朵贴在墙上，试图通过偷听隔壁的谈话，来获取完成工作所需的最基本信息（并非敏感或机密内容）。我的办公邻座却毫不尴尬；他反而抄起一只玻璃杯，凑到墙边当“扩音器”——因为在我们所处的境况下，这竟成了合情合理的反应。

<blockquote>Obviously we need to get the hell out, and we're working on it.</blockquote>

显然，我们必须赶紧逃离这个鬼地方，而我们正在为此努力。

<blockquote>But in the meantime, I'd love to hear readers share their own behavior that made perfect sense in the context of their office dysfunction … and would be horrifying anywhere else. (And advice on keeping your “normal meter” calibrated among that level of chaos is extremely welcome.)</blockquote>

但在此期间，我非常想听听各位读者分享：那些在你们办公室功能失调的语境下显得完全合理、却在任何其他地方都令人毛骨悚然的行为。（另外，关于如何在如此程度的混乱中校准自己的“正常值刻度”，也极其欢迎各位提供建议。）

Readers, this is your moment! What dysfunctional behavior did a toxic office drive you to after warping your normal meter?

各位读者，此刻属于你们！在“正常值刻度”被严重扭曲之后，有毒的办公室曾驱使你做出过哪些失常行为？
