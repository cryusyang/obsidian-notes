<p><strong><a href="https://stevepavlina.com/conscious-growth-club/">Conscious Growth Club</a> </strong>opens for Year 10 signups tomorrow – April 25.</p>



<p>I’ve been shaping the new CGC year with a more robust and flexible structure that I think will serve us really well. The focus is on creating a more supportive, participatory, and adaptive experience – with strong live call formats, personalized support, real connection, and plenty of space for meaningful growth and integration over the course of the year.</p>



<p>If you’ve been feeling drawn to join us, or if you’re considering renewing, tomorrow is the day the invitation goes live.</p>



<p>CGC only opens for a short window once each year for new members to join. This will be our only joining window for 2026 – after this we won&#8217;t open again till April 2027.</p>

## 中文译文

“有意识成长俱乐部”将于明日（4月25日）开启第十期成员报名。

我已为新一期CGC精心设计了一套更为坚实且灵活的架构，相信这将为我们带来更优质的支持体验。本年度的重点在于打造一个更具支持性、参与感与适应性的成长旅程——包括高质量的实时线上共学环节、个性化的陪伴支持、真实而深入的人际联结，并为整年的深度成长与内化整合预留充分空间。

如果你一直感受到加入我们的内在召唤，或正考虑续期参与，那么明日便是正式邀请开启之日。

CGC每年仅开放一次短暂的入会窗口，专供新成员加入。本次即为2026年度唯一入会机会；此后下一次开放将延至2027年4月。
