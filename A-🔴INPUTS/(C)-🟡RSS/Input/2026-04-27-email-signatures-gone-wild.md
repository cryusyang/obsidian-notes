<p>A workplace email signature is normally the most forgettable part of a message &#8212; just a name, a title, a phone number, and maybe a logo dutifully appended by IT. It’s boilerplate by design, stripped of personality and meant to fade into the background.</p>
<p>But when employers give workers more freedom to personalize email signatures, they can quickly get retaliatory, overly personal, or just downright weird. At Slate today, I wrote about some email signatures gone very wild. <a href="https://slate.com/life/2026/04/jobs-email-signature-professional-etiquette-work-company.html">You can read it here</a>.</p>

## 中文译文

职场邮件签名通常是整封邮件中最容易被忽略的部分——无非是姓名、职务、电话号码，或许再加一个由IT部门机械添加的公司Logo。其设计本就意在千篇一律，刻意抹去个性，以便悄然隐入背景。

但当雇主给予员工更多自由，允许其个性化设置邮件签名时，这些签名却可能迅速变得带有对抗意味、过度私人化，甚至干脆怪异至极。今天，《Slate》刊发了我的一篇报道，聚焦那些失控到离谱的邮件签名。您可点击此处阅读全文。
