Our monthly [Happiness Calendar for Educators](https://greatergood.berkeley.edu/images/uploads/GGSC_Education_Happiness_Calendar_May_2026.pdf) is a day-by-day guide to building kinder, happier schools where everyone belongs. This month, **explore the quiet strength of humility** with daily tips from the Greater Good Science Center.

我们每月一期的《教育工作者幸福日历》（[点击查看](https://greatergood.berkeley.edu/images/uploads/GGSC_Education_Happiness_Calendar_May_2026.pdf)）是一份逐日指南，旨在帮助建设更友善、更幸福、让每个人都有归属感的学校。本月，我们将携手“更大善科学中心”（Greater Good Science Center），每日提供实用建议，带您**探索谦逊所蕴含的内在力量**。

We also invite you to join our summer community of practice, Bridging for Belonging: Exploring Empathy, Curiosity, and Intellectual Humility for the Greater Good. This seven session community of practice for educators explores aspirational ways of bringing people together—in the spirit of connection, authentic listening, and healing. [Learn more and register here](https://ggsc.openlearn.berkeley.edu/browse/communities-of-practice/courses/bridging-summer-26).

我们也诚挚邀请您加入今夏的实践者社群：“联结以促归属——为更大善而探索共情、好奇心与智性谦逊”。这一为期七期的教育工作者实践社群，将共同探讨富有理想主义色彩的人际联结方式——以真诚联结、深度倾听与疗愈精神为内核。
