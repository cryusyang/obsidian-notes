We place life’s bets by countless calculations of probability, conscious and unconscious, only to discover over and over how short they fall of the wildest reaches of the possible, which always includes but exceeds the probable.  
我们通过无数有意识与无意识的概率计算，为生命下注；却一再发现，这些计算总是远远不及“可能”之疆域中最狂野的边界——而那边界，永远涵括、又远超“或然”。

It helps to remember that we ourselves are children of improbability, that everything we treasure exists not because it had to, not because it was likely or necessary, but because the universe took a gamble against the staggering odds otherwise.  
铭记这一点颇有裨益：我们自身正是“不可能性”的子嗣；一切我们所珍视之物，并非因其必然如此，亦非因其顺理成章或不可或缺，而仅仅因为宇宙曾以惊人的逆 odds 押下了一注。

> **THE WILDEST BET IS THE WINNING BET**  
> **最狂野的一注，恰是胜出的一注**  
> *by Maria Popova*  
> *作者：玛丽亚·波波娃*

You wouldn’t have bet on it,  
你本不会为此下注——  

the battered rock  
这颗伤痕累累的岩石，  

orbiting a star  
绕着一颗恒星旋转，  

from the discount bin  
它来自宇宙的“清仓区”，  

of the universe,  
那个浩瀚无垠的宇宙；  

wouldn’t have guessed  
你也绝难料到，  

that it would bloom  
它竟会绽放出——  

mitochondria and music,  
线粒体与音乐，  

that it would mushroom  
竟会如蘑菇般涌生出——  

mountains and minds,  
山脉与心智；  

and the hummingbird wing  
还有蜂鸟的翅膀，  

whirring a hundred times faster  
振翅频率比你眨眼快上一百倍，  

than your eye can blink,  
而你的眼睛，  

and your eye that took  
竟花了五亿年光阴，  

five hundred million years  
从三叶虫蜕变为望远镜；  

from trilobite to telescope,  

and the unhurried orange lichen  
还有那从容不迫的橙色地衣，  

growing on the black boulder  
在黝黑巨石上悄然生长，  

two hundred times more slowly  
其速度比脚下板块分离的速率，  

than the tectonic plates beneath  
还要慢上两百倍；  

are drifting apart  

and the marbled orca  
还有那大理石纹路般的虎鲸，  

carrying her dead calf  
驮着她死去的幼崽，  

down the entire edge  
沿着整片大陆的边缘，  

of the continent,  
缓缓游弋，  

carrying the weight  
驮着意识的重量——  

of consciousness  

and consciousness  
而意识本身，  

how it windows  
如何以一扇窗，  

this tenement  
映照出这栋由呼吸与骨骼构成的  

of breath and bone  
暂居之所，  

with wonder,  
并为之注入惊奇；  

how it hovers over everything,  
它如何盘桓于万物之上，  

gigantic and unnecessary,  
庞大而并非必需，  

like music,  
一如音乐，  

like love.  
一如爱。
