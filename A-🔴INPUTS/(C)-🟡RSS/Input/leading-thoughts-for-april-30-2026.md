**I**DEAS shared have the power to expand perspectives, change thinking, and move lives. Here are two ideas for the curious mind to engage with:

分享的思想具有拓展视野、改变思维和影响人生的力量。以下是供求知者深入思考的两个观点：

<center>**I.**</center>

<center>**I.**</center>

**Paul Ingram** on values:

**保罗·英格拉姆**（Paul Ingram）论价值观：

<blockquote>“When you know your values—really know them—you unlock something vital. You get clarity when things are uncertain. You gain confidence when decisions get hard. You find resilience when life throws something unexpected your way. And you create deeper connections with others because you’re leading from a place that’s honest and grounded.”</blockquote>

“当你真正了解自己的价值观——是真正了解——你就开启了一种至关重要的力量：在局势不明时获得清晰认知；在决策艰难时增强信心；在生活抛来意外挑战时展现韧性；并且，因为你以真诚而笃定的方式引领他人，从而与他人建立更深层次的联结。”

Source: [*What Do You Really Stand For? The One Question That Will Transform Your Work and Life*](https://amzn.to/4mfyX2g)

来源：《你真正坚守的是什么？一个将彻底改变你工作与人生的提问》

<center>**II.**</center>

<center>**II.**</center>

**Stanley McCrystal** on the ends justify the means:

**斯坦利·麦克里斯特尔**（Stanley McChrystal）论“目的证明手段正当”：

<blockquote>“It is the ‘end justifies the means’ conundrum. We often can’t be all we want to be without departing from the character we aspire to cultivate. The choice is rarely binary, although we often wish it were.  
But, if we choose an inflexible adherence to certain values, this can prove difficult to pull off within the complexities of the real world. On the other hand, once we depart from our core character, we join the legions of those who have abandoned what matters most.”</blockquote>

“这正是‘目的证明手段正当’这一难题的困境所在。我们常常无法完全实现理想自我，而不偏离自己所努力培养的品格。这种选择极少是非此即彼的二元选项——尽管我们往往希望如此。  
然而，倘若我们僵化地固守某些价值观，便可能难以在现实世界的复杂性中真正践行；反之，一旦我们背离了自身核心品格，便将汇入那支早已抛弃最重要之物的庞大队伍。”

Source: [*On Character: Choices That Define a Life*](https://amzn.to/4gvd3oj)

来源：《论品格：定义人生的抉择》
