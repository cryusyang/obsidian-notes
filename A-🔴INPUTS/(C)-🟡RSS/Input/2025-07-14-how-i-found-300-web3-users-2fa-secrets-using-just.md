> [!example] AI 摘要
> 安全研究员zack0x01在某Web3平台的私有HackerOne项目中，发现一个因第三方服务（genQrcode.com）不当处理2FA注册QR码而导致的高危漏洞。该漏洞使攻击者仅通过解码公开索引的Base64 URL即可获取超300名用户的完整姓名、邮箱及原始TOTP密钥，从而完全绕过双因素认证。问题根源在于将敏感认证凭据交由不可控的外部服务生成，且未加密、无访问控制或时效限制。该漏洞被迅速确认为Critical级别，厂商随即下线暴露链接并强制重置用户2FA，研究者获得丰厚赏金。

<h3>Hey, this is zack0x01 👋</h3><figure><img alt="" src="https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*27gpN0693Kbvc1nwFgUnxA.png" /></figure><p>I’m a bug bounty hunter focused on Web3 apps, and this write-up is for the real ones in the game — the researchers.</p><p>In this post, I’ll walk you through how I discovered a super simple but <strong>high-impact bug</strong> on a Web3 platform (part of a private HackerOne program) that exposed:</p><ul><li>Full names</li><li>Emails</li><li>And raw <strong>TOTP secrets</strong> of <strong>300+ users</strong></li></ul><p>With just one command and a bit of curiosity, I had everything needed to bypass 2FA and fully compromise accounts — no exploits, no bruteforcing, just <strong>poor implementation and a third-party mistake</strong>.</p><h3>🔍 Recon Starts Everything</h3><p>While reviewing 2FA setup, I noticed something sketchy in the frontend logic:</p><pre>&lt;img src=&quot;https://genQrcode.com/{base64}&quot;&gt;</pre><p>The QR code used to enroll in Google Authenticator was being pulled from an external domain — genQrcode.com. The payload? Just a Base64 string.</p><p>This smelled like a lazy shortcut. I dumped the domain through <strong>Waymore</strong> to confirm.</p><h3>🧪 The Command</h3><pre>waymore -i genQrcode.com -o exposed_qrcodes.txt</pre><p>What I got back was wild — <strong>hundreds</strong> of publicly indexed paths, all looking like QR codes.</p><p>I decoded a few Base64 strings from the URLs manually:</p><pre>echo &quot;Sm9obiBEb2U7am9obi5kb2VAZXhhbXBsZS5jb207SkJTV1kzRFBFSFBLM1BY&quot; | base64 -d</pre><p>Output:</p><pre>John Doe;john.doe@example.com;JBSWY3DPEHPK3PXP</pre><p>That’s:</p><ul><li>👤 Full Name</li><li>📧 Email</li><li>🔐 TOTP Secret Key (used for 2FA in Google Authenticator)</li></ul><p>And I wasn’t looking at a single user — this affected <strong>hundreds</strong>, live.</p><h3>🎯 Real-World Exploitation Scenario</h3><p>The app used <strong>TOTP-based 2FA</strong>, and this QR was how users enrolled. So an attacker with this info could:</p><ol><li>Load the TOTP secret into any authenticator app</li><li>Phish or reuse credentials</li><li>Enter valid 6-digit 2FA codes and bypass login protection</li><li>Fully take over user accounts</li></ol><p>On a Web3 platform, this can mean access to <strong>wallets, private keys, linked exchanges</strong>, and more.</p><h3>🧨 Why This Bug Was Dangerous</h3><p>Mistake Result QR generated by 3rd party Data lives outside your control Base64 encoding used No encryption at all No auth / expiry / binding Anyone can access and reuse Indexed by crawlers Recon tools find it instantly</p><p>This was a full-on <strong>2FA bypass chain</strong> — but with zero exploitation required. Just decoding.</p><h3>📆 What Happened Next</h3><p>Date Action 🗓️ :</p><p>May 2025 Reported via private H1 program ✅</p><p>Same day Triaged as Critical 🔐</p><p>Few days later Exposed links shut down, mass 2FA rotation 💰</p><p>June 2025 Rewarded with a solid bounty (undisclosed)</p><h3>💬 Final Thoughts :</h3><p>This bug wasn’t advanced — no need for fuzzers, emulators, or exploits.<br /> It was <strong>pure recon + logic + common sense</strong>.</p><p>So yeah, next time you see a weird external QR or a random Base64 string in the wild:</p><p>👉 <strong>Decode it.</strong><br /> 👉 <strong>Check who’s hosting it.</strong><br /> 👉 <strong>Assume nothing is protected unless proven.</strong></p><p><strong>The lesson here?</strong></p><blockquote><em>Third-party dependencies are often the weakest link — and when they’re in your auth flow, it’s game over.</em></blockquote><p>Keep hunting, keep digging, and don’t sleep on the simple stuff.</p><p>See you at the top.<br /> <strong>— zack0x01</strong></p><p>Feel free to check out my youtube channel : <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@ZACK0X01">https://www.youtube.com/@ZACK0X01</a></p><img alt="" height="1" src="https://medium.com/_/stat?event=post.clientViewed&amp;referrerSource=full_rss&amp;postId=13fa202393e0" width="1" />
