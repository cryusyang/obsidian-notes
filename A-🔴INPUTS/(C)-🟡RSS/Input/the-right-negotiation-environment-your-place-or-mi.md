Everyone knows the three rules of real estate: “Location! Location! Location!” When it comes to making deals, choosing the right [negotiation environment](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/what-is-negotiation/) can be just as critical. The setting you select can shape the tone of the discussion, influence the process, and ultimately affect the final outcome. In [dealmaking](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/dealmaking/), the question “Your place or mine?” is never a simple one. It calls for thoughtful consideration of the negotiation ahead and the dynamics you hope to create.

人人都知道房地产行业的三大法则：“地段！地段！地段！”而在达成交易的过程中，选择恰当的[谈判环境](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/what-is-negotiation/)同样至关重要。你所选定的场所会影响讨论的基调、左右谈判进程，并最终左右最终结果。在[交易谈判](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/dealmaking/)中，“在你那儿谈，还是在我这儿谈？”这一问题从来都不简单——它要求你审慎思考即将展开的谈判，以及你希望营造的互动氛围。

## Choosing the Right Negotiation Environment

## 如何选择恰当的谈判环境

**Negotiating at your place.** Like athletes seeking the home-field advantage, most people prefer to negotiate on their own territory. Meeting at your office offers many perceived benefits. First, you gain the advantage of familiarity with the negotiating environment. You know where everything is located, from reliable secretarial services to secure areas for private consultation. Your counterpart, on the other hand, runs the risk of unfamiliarity.

**在己方场所谈判。** 就像运动员力求主场优势一样，多数人倾向于在自己的地盘上进行谈判。在你自己的办公室会面，带来诸多被普遍认可的好处：首先，你对谈判环境了如指掌——从可靠的秘书服务到可供私密磋商的安全区域，一切尽在掌握；而你的谈判对手则面临不熟悉环境的风险。

Negotiating at home also allows you to control the negotiation environment, including the selection and arrangement of the meeting room, the seating of participants at the [bargaining table](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/bargaining-table/), and the nature and timing of hospitality and social events. Playing host gives you the opportunity to impress the other team with your company’s resources. Finally, negotiating at home is cheaper, eliminating travel costs and saving executive time.

在己方场所谈判还使你能够掌控整个谈判环境：包括会议室的选择与布置、参与人员在[谈判桌](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/bargaining-table/)上的座次安排，以及招待与社交活动的形式与时间安排。作为东道主，你有机会向对方团队展示本公司的雄厚资源。最后，在己方场所谈判成本更低——可省去差旅开支，并节约高管宝贵的时间。

Whereas host negotiators can usually continue to handle other job demands while participating in talks, a visiting negotiator cannot do the same. Negotiating at home also spares you the pressures of being away from your family, friends, and daily routines. Visiting executives may tend to make a deal or break off talks more quickly than if they were negotiating on their own turf—often to their disadvantage.

相较之下，作为东道主的谈判者通常能在参与谈判的同时兼顾其他工作事务，而来访的谈判者却难以做到这一点。在己方场所谈判还能让你免于承受远离家人、朋友及日常生活的压力。外派高管往往倾向于更快地达成协议或中止谈判——而这常常对其自身不利。

**Negotiating at your counterpart’s place.** At first blush, negotiating on your counterpart’s turf seems to offer only disadvantages: travel can be costly; the negotiation environment is unfamiliar and uncontrollable; lines of communication to your home office may be slow, uncertain, and insecure; and, when traveling a great distance, you face the psychological pressures of being away from home.

**在对方场所谈判。** 初看之下，在对方的地盘上谈判似乎只有劣势：差旅成本高昂；谈判环境陌生且不可控；与总部之间的沟通渠道可能迟缓、不确定甚至不安全；若远途出行，你还需承受远离家乡带来的心理压力。

On the other hand, when you’re the seller, often the only way to bring your product or service to the other side’s attention is to show up at his office. The choice of a negotiating site also has symbolic value. By entering your counterpart’s territory, you show your serious intent and strong desire to make a deal—factors that can be invaluable in persuading him to sign a contract.

但另一方面，当你身为卖方时，要让对方注意到你的产品或服务，最直接的方式往往就是亲自登门拜访其办公室。谈判地点的选择也具有象征意义：进入对方领地，即表明你态度认真、诚意十足，这种姿态对于说服对方签署合同极为关键。

The most important reason to negotiate on the other side’s territory is that it gives you opportunities to learn. When making deals, executives are laying the foundation for a continuing relationship—which depends crucially on how well parties know each other. A vital purpose of any [business negotiation](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/business-negotiation/) is to allow both sides to learn about each other, their businesses, and the conditions in which they must operate. In this respect, the home field *doesn’t* hold an advantage.

在对方领地谈判最重要的原因在于：它为你提供了学习的机会。达成交易的过程，实则是为持续合作关系奠定基础——而这种关系能否稳固，关键取决于双方彼此了解的程度。任何一场[商务谈判](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/business-negotiation/)的核心目的之一，正是让双方深入了解彼此、各自的业务，以及各自运营所处的现实条件。就此而言，主场**并不具备优势**。
