<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Challenge</h2>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A multinational consumer goods company operating in hundreds of countries manages a highly complex data and technology ecosystem spanning thousands of systems. The organization struggled with persistent “metadata pockets” and limited enterprise-wide standardization, making it difficult to connect knowledge and information across disparate business units and geographies. The company’s leadership lacked an understanding of potential return on investment (ROI), making it challenging to justify investment, resulting in semantic use cases confined to isolated internal pilots. AI adoption was similarly constrained; solutions were not designed around end-user workflows, and executives lacked a clear, prioritized path for where to invest next to deliver measurable value.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">These challenges created friction across core business processes. Analysts spent significant time locating, validating, and procuring data before analysis could even begin, slowing decision-making and increasing the cost of reporting and insight generation. Critical </span><strong><a href="https://enterprise-knowledge.com/what-is-a-knowledge-asset/">knowledge assets</a></strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> were difficult to find, tacit knowledge was frequently lost during organizational or individual transitions, and AI initiatives failed to gain traction due to weak organizational context and unclear use cases. This contributed to overlapping technology investments and avoidable vendor lock-in.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The company engaged Enterprise Knowledge (EK) to help assess its current semantic maturity, identify gaps against industry best practices, and define a roadmap and supporting business case to reach its target state maturity. They also needed a practical path toward a “North Star” architecture that would enable scalable semantic solutions tied directly to tangible organizational benefits.</span></p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Solution</h2>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">EK conducted a six-week assessment using our proprietary </span><a href="https://enterprise-knowledge.com/ai-readiness-assessment-benchmarking-strategy/"><span style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Semantic Layer Maturity Benchmark</strong></span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, built on insights from over 150 client engagements, to evaluate the client’s current state, across Vision, Strategy, ROI, Knowledge Assets, Semantic Models, Tooling &amp; Architecture, and Operating Model. EK also delivered a North Star Architecture and a Semantic Design Framework to align semantic capabilities with real business workflows and measurable outcomes.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Within that six-week period, EK completed an accelerated two-week stakeholder engagement effort to gather input from business and technical teams through interviews, documentation review, and analysis of architecture artifacts. This discovery work ensured the assessment reflected real business workflows, end-user requirements, and implementation constraints.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Based on these findings, EK defined three priority use cases to anchor the current and target states, ensuring the roadmap focused on practical next steps that could demonstrate value quickly while supporting long-term scalability.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"><br /></span><span style="font-weight: 400;"><br /></span><span style="font-weight: 400;">At the end of the engagement, EK delivered a solution including: </span></p>
<ul>

<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">A Semantic Design Framework.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">A North Star Architecture for an integrated, future-ready data fabric and enterprise knowledge graph.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">A Scalable Implementation Roadmap, including a phased evolution plan to progress toward the North Star architecture.</span></li>
</ul>

## 中文译文

挑战

一家业务遍及全球数百个国家的跨国消费品公司，运营着一个高度复杂的、横跨数千个系统的数据与技术生态系统。该公司长期面临“元数据孤岛”问题，且企业级标准化程度有限，导致不同业务单元及地理区域之间的知识与信息难以有效贯通。公司高层管理者对潜在投资回报率（ROI）缺乏清晰认知，因而难以合理论证相关投入的必要性，致使语义化应用案例仅局限于若干孤立的内部试点项目。人工智能（AI）的落地同样受限：现有解决方案未能围绕终端用户的实际工作流程设计，高管层亦缺乏一条清晰、可优先执行的路径，以明确下一步应在何处投入资源，从而切实交付可衡量的价值。

上述挑战在核心业务流程中引发了多重摩擦。分析师在开展分析工作前，需耗费大量时间查找、验证和获取所需数据，严重拖慢决策节奏，并推高报告编制与洞察生成的成本。关键知识资产难以被快速定位；组织架构调整或人员流动时，隐性知识往往随之流失；而AI项目则因组织上下文薄弱、应用场景模糊而迟迟难以落地。这些问题进一步导致技术投资重复叠加，以及本可避免的供应商锁定风险。

为此，该公司聘请Enterprise Knowledge（EK）协助评估其当前语义化成熟度，识别与行业最佳实践之间的差距，并制定实现目标成熟度的路线图及配套商业论证。同时，公司亟需一条切实可行的路径，构建面向未来的“北极星”架构，使可扩展的语义化解决方案能直接支撑组织层面可感知的实际效益。

解决方案

EK采用自主研发的《语义层成熟度基准评估模型》（Semantic Layer Maturity Benchmark），开展为期六周的全面评估。该模型基于逾150个客户项目的实践经验，从愿景、战略、投资回报率（ROI）、知识资产、语义模型、工具与架构、运营模式七大维度，系统评估客户当前状态。此外，EK还为客户定制交付了“北极星”架构蓝图及语义化设计框架，确保语义能力与真实业务流程及可衡量成果紧密对齐。

在六周期间，EK同步开展了为期两周的加速式干系人协作工作：通过深度访谈、文档审阅及架构制品分析等方式，广泛收集业务与技术团队的意见。此项调研工作确保评估结果真实反映一线业务流程、终端用户需求及实际落地约束条件。

基于上述发现，EK甄选出三项高优先级用例，作为锚定现状与目标状态的核心支点，确保所制定的路线图聚焦于务实可行的后续步骤——既能快速展现价值，又可支撑长期规模化演进。项目结束时，EK交付了一整套解决方案，包括：

- 一份《基准评估与差距分析报告》，全面揭示客户当前语义化成熟度及信息管理实践现状；  
- 一套《语义化设计框架》；  
- 一份面向集成化、面向未来的“北极星”架构蓝图，涵盖数据织网（Data Fabric）与企业级知识图谱（Enterprise Knowledge Graph）；  
- 一份《可扩展实施路线图》，含分阶段演进计划，明确通向“北极星”架构的具体路径。

EK的独特优势

EK依托自有《语义层成熟度基准评估模型》，精准评估了该组织语义能力的发展水平；并将评估结果与AI就绪度洞察相结合，辅以对目标内容与元数据的结构化评估，确认其基础资产已具备支撑AI项目的能力。

本次合作的关键环节之一，是向高层管理者汇报评估发现，并协同五位业务负责人共同验证优先级用例、统筹跨职能考量、推动实施共识的建立。这一过程确保所有建议均根植于真实业务需求，真正契合客户价值诉求。

本次合作尤为注重将语义化举措与可量化指标挂钩。EK将推荐的各项能力，精准对应至五大核心业务指标，清晰阐明其如何减少人工操作负担、提升资产复用率、强化决策质量。此外，EK还提供了运营模式优化建议，以保障规模化落地的可持续性，内容涵盖权责归属机制、治理流程设计、采纳赋能策略，以及在消除各业务单元重复建设的同时，保留必要的区域灵活性。

成果

本次合作不仅帮助客户清晰把握自身现状，更在管理层与各关键干系人之间建立起关于语义化能力与知识管理未来图景的统一、详尽共识。项目成功凝聚了转型行动的集体意愿，交付了一系列与可衡量成果挂钩的商业论证、一份立足行业最佳实践的差距分析报告，以及一条分阶段推进的清晰路径——助力高层管理者信心十足地做出战略性投入决策，明确下一阶段应优先启动的重点方向。
