<p>Consider the social ranking of who is how much of an intellectual. Think of this ranking as made by a weighted average of the opinions of other intellectuals. If we look at how this weighting changes across intellectual levels, there will be a median level, where half of the weight comes from opinions above that level, and half below. </p><p>I asked <em><a href="https://chatgpt.com/share/69ee1eea-e120-83ea-9fd1-e6b10bc7aa72">ChatGPT</a></em> (5.5) and <em><a href="https://claude.ai/share/4a254291-ecb6-46bb-8a3b-b853036827f0">Claude</a></em> (4.7) to give percentile estimates for the median level who judges who are the very best intellectuals, for the West in various years. They gave median 99%,99.5% for year 1000, median 96%,97% for year 1750, median 93%,90% for 1900, and median 88%,80% for 2025. </p><p>We have thus seen an increasing populism in who among us judges who are our very best intellectuals. Which is plausibly a source of intellectual decay. Especially as it is often noted that we usually find it hard to distinguish between mental quality levels above our own.</p>

## 中文译文

试想一下，社会对“谁在多大程度上算得上知识分子”所作的层级排序。可将这一排序理解为其他知识分子意见的加权平均值。若我们观察这种权重分配如何随知识分子自身层级而变化，便会发现一个中位层级：该层级之上与之下的人所提供的意见权重各占一半。

我请ChatGPT（版本5.5）和Claude（版本4.7）分别估算：在不同时期的西方社会中，判断“谁是最顶尖知识分子”的那批人，其自身在知识分子总体中的百分位数中位值是多少？二者给出的结果如下：公元1000年，中位值分别为99%和99.5%；1750年为96%和97%；1900年为93%和90%；2025年则为88%和80%。

由此可见，判定“谁是我们最杰出的知识分子”的主体，正日益呈现民粹化趋势。这种趋势很可能正是知识衰微的一个根源——尤其值得留意的是，人们通常难以准确分辨高于自身心智水准的差异。
